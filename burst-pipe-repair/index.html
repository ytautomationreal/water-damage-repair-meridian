Warning Signs

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Water Damage

Sudden drop in water pressure

A split line is diverting water where it doesn't belong.

Water stain spreading on a ceiling or wall

The leak is active above or inside — it will not dry itself.

Sound of running water with everything off

Trust your ears; check the meter to confirm.

Frost on exposed pipes

A frozen pipe is a burst pipe waiting for the thaw.

Bulging drywall or peeling paint

Water is pooling inside the wall cavity.

No water from a faucet in winter

Frozen line — shut off the main before it thaws and splits.

Equipment We Bring for Burst Pipe Water Damage

Professional drying is a measured process, not a fan in the corner. The machines and instruments that show up on this job:

Pin and pinless meters plus infrared cameras find hidden water behind walls and under floors — and verify dry before anything is closed up.

Air Movers & Axial Fans
