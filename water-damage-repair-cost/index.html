What raises your bill

  • Time. Water wicks into drywall and insulation by the hour. A same-day call often means drying; a three-day-old call means demolition.
  • Category. Clean water (Category 1) is cheapest. Sewage (Category 3) requires material removal and disinfection.
  • Finished spaces. Carpet, hardwood, built-ins, and trim multiply labor and materials.
  • Hidden spread. Water inside wall cavities, under tile, and in the crawl space needs opening up to dry — documented, meter-verified opening, not guesswork.

How to keep costs down

  • Shut off the water source immediately — know where your main valve is before you need it.
  • Call within the first hours. Same-day extraction is the single biggest cost saver.
  • Move contents out of wet areas; photograph everything for your claim.
  • Don't run household fans for days hoping — if moisture readings would still be high, mold is already on the clock.

Insurance note

Sudden events (burst pipes, appliance failures, sump failures with the right endorsement) are commonly covered. Standard policies do not automatically cover sump-pump failure or sewer backup. Our estimates are line-item formatted the way adjusters process them. Details: insurance claims guide.
